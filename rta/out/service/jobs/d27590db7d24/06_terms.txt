Terms and Conditions of Online Prebooking for Intercity Bus Users

1. Departure Schedule:
The bus departs on time, customers are required to be present at the boarding station 30 minutes before the departure time.

2. Bus Safety for Children:
• Children aged 0-7 years are not allowed to board the bus unless accompanied by a guardian.
• Children aged 8-12 years require a guardian’s permit to board the bus without a guardian.
• Tickets must be purchased for children above the age of 5 years. Children under 5 years can travel free of charge, but will not have a reserved seat.

3. Ticket Requirements:
Tickets must be presented at the time of boarding.

4. General Policies:
• No pets are allowed on board any of our buses.
• No food or beverages are allowed on board the bus.
• RTA reserves the right to prohibit any customer from commuting due to bad behavior.
• Passengers must always adhere to RTA’s Terms & Conditions of Carriage (https://www.rta.aehttps://www.rta.aehttps://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/public-transport/dubaibus).
• RTA reserves the right to cancel or change the departure time. In the case of a cancelled scheduled trip, a refund or exchange can be processed for affected customers.

5. Luggage Policy:
• Luggage compartments are available only for Abu Dhabi and Al Ain routes, allowing commuters to store their luggage/belongings in designated compartments.
• E-scooters are not allowed on board the bus or in the luggage compartment due to safety concerns.
• Perishable goods are not allowed on board or in luggage storage.

Each passenger is allowed to bring two large bags for the cargo hold, with each bag not exceeding the dimensions of 80 × 50 × 35 cm and a weight of 30 kg.
In addition, a small hand luggage or backpack is permitted inside the bus with dimensions not exceeding 50 × 35 × 20 cm and a weight not exceeding 10 kg.
The Roads and Transport Authority (RTA) reserves the right to refuse any luggage that exceeds these limits.

Large Luggage for the Bus Cargo Hold:
Standard Dimensions: 80 cm (length) × 50 cm (width) × 35 cm (depth)
Allowed Weight: 30 kg per bag.

Small Hand Luggage (Backpack or Handbag) for Use Inside the Bus:
Standard Dimensions: 50 cm (length) × 35 cm (width) × 20 cm (depth)
Allowed Weight: 10 kg.

6. Advance Booking:
Pre-booking can be made up to 30 days before the scheduled departure time.

7. Cancellation Policy:
• 0-72 hours before departure: Non-Refundable allowed.
• More than 72 hours before departure: Fully Refundable allowed.

8. Exchange Policy:
• 0-72 hours before departure: No exchange allowed.
• More than 72 hours before departure: Full exchange allowed.
